Information for new and returning visitors

Before your visit

Jam Hearts welcomes visitors Tuesday through Friday from 12:00–7:00 p.m. Saturday hours are 10:00 a.m.–7:00 p.m., while Sunday hours are 11:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. The center is closed on Monday.

Arrival information

The main entrance faces Willow Avenue. Visitors requiring the accessible entrance should enter from the east parking area.

Helpful details

  • General visitor questions may be directed to hello@jamhearts.org.
  • Visitor parking is behind the east building.
  • The accessible entrance is located beside the east parking area.
