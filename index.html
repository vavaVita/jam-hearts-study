Jam Hearts Website Study

You will use a fictional arts-center website to find six pieces of information.

Please complete the tasks in order. Do not use your browser’s Find function, search engine, or another device.

You may navigate through the website normally. Once you find an answer, enter it in the task field and continue.

Please complete the study in one sitting.

  1. Find the annual cost of a student membership.
  2. Find the registration deadline for the Creative Writing Lab.
  3. Find where visitors should park.
  4. Find the room used for Photography Foundations.
  5. Find the closing date of the Forms in Motion exhibition.
  6. Find the email address used for accessibility requests.
